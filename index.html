LETTERS!

The shout-it-out party game for 2 to 30 players.

Quick 5 to 15 minute rounds. One phone. Zero setup.

How to Play

  1. 1
    Pick a Judge. The Judge holds the phone, enters everyone's names, and starts the game. (Playing Casual? Skip the names and hit Quick Play.)
  2. 2
    Read the card. Each card shows a random letter and two topics. The Judge reads the letter and one of the topics out loud.
  3. 3
    Shout it out! Everyone races to yell a word that fits the topic and starts with the letter. Letter D for Dessert? Donut!
  4. 4
    Award the Letter. The Judge taps the name of the first player with a correct answer. Each card won is one Letter.
  5. 5
    First to the target wins. Play to 5, 10, 15 or 20 Letters. Or play Casual mode: no scores, just tap Next card and keep laughing.
